Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Sep 21, 2025

Overview

This Chrome extension PII 360 is designed to detect sensitive information (PII) in documents locally on the user’s device. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Data Collection and Use

The Extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from users.

All processing, including machine learning model inference, takes place locally on your device.

No user content (text, PDFs, or other files) is ever sent to external servers.

Data Sharing

Since no user data is collected, no data is shared with third parties.

Permissions

The Extension requests the following permissions solely to provide its functionality:

storage & unlimitedStorage: Used to cache machine learning models locally (~2 GB) to improve performance and enable offline use.

Changes to this Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be posted in the Chrome Web Store listing.
